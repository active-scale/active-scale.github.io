These are real video excerpts released on the dataset publishers' pages,
not still-image animations, simulated rollouts, or ActiveScale/AMP task footage.
They illustrate the source datasets, not the exact episodes selected for
ActiveScale training. Original motion speed is preserved.

Output encoding
640x360 H.264, yuv420p, 24 fps, silent, fast-start MP4.
Original aspect ratios are preserved; 4:3 sources have narrow dark sidebars.
Each JPG poster comes from second 1 of the matching output clip.
