The robot montage uses eight distinct video excerpts from official AgiBot World
and RoboCOIN previews, plus an AgiBot highlight reel. The selected-source stage
uses AgiBot World/RoboCOIN highlight reels and the author's AMP teleoperation
video. These are dataset illustrations, not the exact episodes used in training.
Original URLs, excerpt ranges and processing details: robot-video-sources.txt.
Runtime asset mapping: robot-video-manifest.json.

The human motion uses the existing licensed MPFB/MakeHuman rig with a staged
head-and-wrist reach toward a stationary mug. Labeled local XYZ frames and motion
trails illustrate the supervision signals; this is not a recorded data replay.

The grasp-offset scene uses the vendor PiPER gripper mesh and a stationary bottle.
The gripper closes beside the target to illustrate the preliminary human-only
observation. The illustrated displacement is not a measured experimental value.
