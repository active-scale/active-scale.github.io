This site uses simplified Tracer 2 chassis, Cobot Magic frame and Orbbec DaBai visual meshes derived from that package. COLLADA transforms were baked into STL; vertices were quantized for web delivery. The AMP frame additionally removes the display and upper upright to illustrate the author's hardware assembly. These edits are an illustrative reconstruction, not vendor measurements of AMP.
