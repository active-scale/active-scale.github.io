# Transfer demo avatar

The rigged human mesh is the [Innerscene 53-bone human base mesh](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/human-base-mesh-with-editable-53-bone-rig-8e7c8ab1), adapted from MakeHuman / MPFB. Innerscene releases this asset under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). ActiveScale applies workwear colors and a procedural reach trajectory for the interactive demonstration.
